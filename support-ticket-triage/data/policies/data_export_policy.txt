NovaDesk Data Export and Deletion Policy (v1.2)
Last updated: 2026-03-05

Workspace owners can export tickets and comments as CSV from Settings > Export.
Exports are prepared within 24 hours. Agents must not email raw exports to
personal Gmail or Yahoo addresses.

Account deletion requests from workspace owners are completed within 30 days.
During those 30 days, billing still applies unless the owner cancels the plan.
Deleted workspaces cannot be restored after deletion is finished.
