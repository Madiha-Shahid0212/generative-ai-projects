NovaDesk Refund Policy (v2.1)
Last updated: 2026-01-10

Cash refunds are available only within 14 calendar days of the first payment.
After day 14, support may offer account credit only. Agents must not promise
cash refunds after day 14.

Annual plans are refundable for cash only in the first 14 days and only if
the workspace has fewer than 3 active seats. Setup fees are never refundable.

Account credit expires 90 days after it is issued and cannot be transferred
to another customer.
