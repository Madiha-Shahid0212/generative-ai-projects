NovaDesk Access and Response SLA Policy (v1.4)
Last updated: 2026-02-01

Password reset emails are sent within 5 minutes for verified accounts.
If a reset email is missing, agents must first confirm the login email
and ask the customer to check spam before escalating.

Priority levels:
- Critical: full product outage affecting all customers
- High: billing charge errors or locked-out admins on paid plans
- Medium: single-user bugs that still allow basic work
- Low: how-to questions and feature ideas

Critical tickets must get a first reply within 1 hour on business days.
Low tickets may wait up to 2 business days for a first reply.
